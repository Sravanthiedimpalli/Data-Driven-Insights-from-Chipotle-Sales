# Data-Driven-Insights-from-Chipotle-Sales

Performed end-to-end exploratory data analysis on 4,600+ Chipotle restaurant orders. Identified most popular menu items, high-revenue choices, and purchasing patterns. Delivered actionable insights for targeted marketing and pricing optimization using Python, pandas, and data visualization.

## Overview

Analyzed 4,622 Chipotle food orders using Python (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn) to extract actionable business insights. The project includes data cleaning, summary statistics, sales and revenue analysis, and data visualizations to uncover popular items and customer preferences.

## Key Insights

- Identified top-selling menu items and high-frequency order choices
- Visualized item quantities, revenue trends, and customer order patterns
- Delivered recommendations for optimizing menu design, pricing, and marketing strategies

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values, converted price data to numeric, removed duplicates
2. **Exploratory Analysis:** Summary statistics, top items and choices, revenue and order distributions
3. **Data Visualization:** Used bar charts, pie charts, and histograms for sales and revenue trends
4. **Business Insights:** Recommendations for promotions, product bundling, and menu optimization

## Tools Used

- Python (Jupyter Notebook)
- pandas
- matplotlib
- seaborn

## Conclusion

This project provided powerful business insights from Chipotle’s order data through thorough cleaning, exploration, and visualization. Key findings reveal that a small number of menu items and choice customizations drive the majority of sales and revenue. These results highlight clear opportunities for targeted marketing, focused promotions, and product bundling to enhance customer satisfaction and increase profit. Data-driven strategies based on these insights can help Chipotle fine-tune its menu offerings and pricing to better match customer demand.
